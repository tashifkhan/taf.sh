# Paisa product video

Paisa keeps an expense record on the user's device. Android can turn supported bank SMS into transactions. Users can also enter expenses manually or connect their own AI provider to parse payment text, photos and PDFs into drafts.

The revised walkthrough leads with the phone and shows each core feature there. The last chapter demonstrates the unfolded foldable, an Android tablet and the local web app.

The browser footage still comes from the local Kotlin Multiplatform app. At the user's request, presentation chrome and the closing URL read `paisa.tashif.codes`.

## Scene order

1. Capture. A bank SMS creates a transaction. Open its parsed details and original message. Show Add with AI parsing payment text into two drafts, changing their account on request and saving both. Follow with a quick manual coffee entry and save.
2. Review. Merchant search, category analytics, monthly budgets, separate accounts and optional backup.
3. Appearance and privacy. Material You wallpaper colors, 31 fixed palettes, dark mode, AMOLED black, expressive motion, high contrast and floating navigation. Hide amounts, then show the actual app lock gate.
4. Screens. Unfolded list and detail, native tablet detail and analytics, then web search and detail.
5. Close. Paisa icon, requested URL, phone, foldable, tablet and web.

Bank SMS uses on-device parsers. It does not use an AI model. Add with AI is a separate, optional flow: the user adds their own provider key, picks from the model catalogue and can order fallback models in a ladder. Keep the copy provider-neutral. Never imply that an unconfigured model produced a result or that cloud backup ran without a destination.

## Artwork

Nineteen App Store-style portrait panels cover the overview, SMS parsing, SMS source, AI drafts, manual entry, analytics, budgets, accounts, merchant search, Material You, customization, AMOLED black, hide amounts, app lock, foldable, tablet, web, backup and the device family. Each has a separately composed wide gallery version. No footer or panel numbering appears on the artwork. Use the Paisa launcher icon and Bricolage Grotesque throughout. The captured app retains its own type.
