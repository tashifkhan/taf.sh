list, the setup guide is on a board, and the day has personal tasks too.
"The work is scattered. Your next step doesn't have to be."

1. Decide what needs doing. Show iOS due-date groups, then the Android list.
   Filter the release tag and search the same sample release work. Keep both
   native interfaces visible long enough to read.
2. Turn a task into a next step. Open notes and a checklist on iOS, then edit
   the priority on Android. Show the resulting native UI state after the change.
3. Keep the project moving. Open the Deck release board on Android, then its
   card details on iOS. Tie the setup-guide card back to the release work.
4. Choose how you work. Pair each platform's appearance screen. Briefly show
   provider configuration and explain reviewable AI drafts without pretending
   to demonstrate an AI result.

Close with both phones and "Pick up the next task. On your phone."
Show the source URL. Name iOS and Android only in the opening scene. Keep source
integration and storage explanations short so the film follows the work.
