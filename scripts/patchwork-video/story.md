# Patchwork product video

Patchwork is for people whose tasks live in several systems. Before Patchwork,
they check CalDAV lists, GitHub issues, Jira and Nextcloud Deck separately.
The native app brings those tasks and boards to one phone interface.

The loop is connect a source, see what needs doing, open the task or card,
make a change, and send it back to the source. Local lists also work without
an account. Native credentials stay in device secure storage.

Four chapters prove that loop.

1. Plan the day. Group tasks by due date, filter lists and tags, and search.
2. Keep the details with the task. Notes, checklists, priorities and due dates.
3. Follow work across a board. Browse Nextcloud Deck columns and open cards.
4. Choose how it works. Integration settings, AI provider configuration and appearance.

Both iOS and Android are native builds. The current preview records the real
screens on the iPhone 18 Pro simulator. Android recording is paused at the
user's request after installation failed because emulator storage was full.
Task and Deck footage uses the
local synthetic CalDAV/Deck server in demo-server.py. Keep a visible sample-data
label on those scenes. Settings footage demonstrates configuration, not a live
GitHub/Jira connection or an AI result. Do not imply that an unconfigured model
has generated tasks. Slack, Linear, web and macOS are outside this video.

Use no invented usage numbers. The meaningful counts are two native phone
platforms and the four supported remote sources documented in the repository.

The opening shows the iOS app and names the purpose. A short diagram explains
how sources connect before the first feature clip. Four chapters follow, mixing
phone footage with concise copy. Close on the repository URL and say Android
capture is pending.

Visual identity comes from Patchwork's native theme and app assets. Use its
warm off-white splash background, plum foreground and coral accent, dark panels
around the dark phone captures, and the actual Patchwork icon. Use Archivo for
all artwork and video typography. Keep phone text
large enough to read. Use the product-video skill's deterministic HTML engine,
frame sampling and synthesized soundtrack. Export the film and poster under
public/reel, and register Patchwork in the existing taf.sh reel.
